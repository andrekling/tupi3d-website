Tupi3D Code of Conduct

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Tupi3D lets people model together in the same space, talk to each other and show their work. These rules apply to everyone in a shared session. They sit alongside Meta's Code of Conduct for Virtual Experiences, which also applies on Meta headsets.

Be someone others want to work with

  • Treat the people in your session with respect.
  • Respect personal space. Do not crowd, follow or block someone who has moved away or asked you to stop.
  • Ask before changing or deleting someone else's work.

Not allowed

  • Harassment, bullying, threats or hate speech, in voice, text, drawings or models.
  • Sexual content or sexual advances, and anything that sexualises minors.
  • Sharing someone's private information, or recording or streaming a session without telling the people in it.
  • Content that is illegal, or that promotes violence or self-harm.
  • Impersonating another person, or getting round a mute, removal or ban.

If something goes wrong

  • Mute or hide the person for yourself in the People list.
  • Tell the host. The host of a session can mute someone for everyone, remove them or ban their device.
  • Report it. Press the menu button on your controller and choose Report, use the Report button next to the person's name, or write to andrekling@gmail.com. Say what happened, when, and the name the person was using.
  • Leave the session at any time from the Collab menu.

What we do with a report

We read every report and aim to answer within 7 days. Sessions are private and run by their host, and we cannot see or hear what happens in them, so we rely on what you tell us. Where a report is confirmed we can block a device from using our relay server. Serious cases may be passed to Meta or to the authorities.

Hosts

If you host a session you are responsible for it. Share your room code only with people you want there, and use mute, remove and ban when you need to.
