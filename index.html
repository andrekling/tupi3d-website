Modelling

Real polygon modelling, not a toy.

Pick vertices, edges and faces, and pull them where they need to go. The tools you know from desktop 3D, made for VR.

  • Extrude, inset, bevel, knife, loop cut, slide, merge, bridge, loft and grid fill
  • Modifiers: Boolean, Bevel, Mirror, Array, Subdivision, Lattice and more
  • Shapes that stay editable, a work plane, snapping and measuring tools
  • Undo for every step
Painting

Paint right on the surface.

Hold the airbrush like a real one: the spray follows your distance and how hard you press.

  • Airbrush, brushes and smudge, with pressure
  • Stencils and French curves
  • Layers, and maps baked from the object: AO, curvature, edges, thickness
  • Vertex colours and a shared palette
  • PBR workflow ready
Materials

Node materials you can reach into.

Build materials from nodes around you, and watch them change live on your model.

  • Noise, Voronoi, bricks and other generators
  • Image textures, triplanar mapping and PBR outputs
  • Carry a material onto any object, like a swatch
  • Material Maker compatible
Rendering

Cycles, built in.

The Cycles path tracer, Blender's renderer, is built in. Tupi3D rendered this picture.

  • Physical cameras with lenses and depth of field
  • Sky images for light, and a live preview
  • Render on the headset, or in seconds on your PC with Saci

Your PC, a hop away.

Tupi3D's companion for your computer. Your monitors appear in the headset, files move both ways, renders run on your graphics card, and your headset view shows on the PC, ready for OBS.
