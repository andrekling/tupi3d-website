Saci

Your PC, a hop away from your headset.

Saci is Tupi3D's companion for your computer. Named for the one-legged trickster of Brazilian legend, who hops from place to place, it carries you between the headset and your desk.

Download for WindowsWindows 10 or 11, 64-bit · zip Download for Linuxx86-64, X11 desktop · tar.gz

Beta. Free. Saci only answers headsets on your own home network. All versions

  • Your screensEvery monitor on its own board in the headset: point to move the mouse, the trigger clicks, the stick scrolls.
  • Your projectsScreenshots and videos from the headset land on the PC by themselves; whole projects go either way.
  • Your graphics cardCycles renders and heavy modelling steps run on the PC and come back in seconds.
  • Your viewWhat the headset sees, in a window on the PC — ready for OBS.

Getting started

  1. Unzip Saci somewhere it can stay and run Saci.exe (on Linux, Saci.x86_64). Windows may warn that the program is not signed yet: choose More info, then Run anyway. Allow it on private networks when the firewall asks.
  2. In the headset, open Tupi3D and press Saci on Guarani's Project ring.
  3. In AR, just look at the code in Saci's window. Otherwise pick your PC and type the password Saci shows. After the first time it connects by itself.

On Linux Saci needs ffmpeg (with ffplay) and python3 from your distribution. Saci used to be called Tupi Desk; your pairings come along when you switch.
